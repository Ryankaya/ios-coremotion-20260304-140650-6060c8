# Posture Help: Support

Need help? Email **‹contact email›** and we'll get back to you.

## Getting started

1. Open Posture Help and follow the short welcome tour.
2. Sit upright, hold your phone where you normally read, and tap **Start Calibration**.
3. Tap **Start Session** on the Today tab and use your phone as usual.

## Frequently asked questions

**I'm not getting reminders.**
Make sure notifications are allowed (Settings → Notifications → Posture Help) and that a session is running. Reminders pause automatically while your phone is locked, in a pocket, or resting on a table. Check the Smart pause section on the Today tab.

**I get reminders too often (or not often enough).**
Go to **Settings → Sensitivity** and pick Relaxed, Balanced or Strict, or open **Fine-tune** to set the lean angle, how long you can lean before a nudge, and how often reminders repeat.

**The angle looks wrong.**
Recalibrate from **Settings → Recalibrate upright posture** (end your session first) while sitting upright and holding your phone the way you usually do.

**Does it work while I'm using other apps?**
Yes. While a session is running, reminders arrive as notifications in other apps.

**How do I add widgets?**
Long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap **Edit → Add Widget**, and search for **Posture Help**. You can also add the Posture Session and Calibrate controls to Control Center.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. Everything stays on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
