# Posture Help: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Publisher: Austin Ventures LLC_

Posture Help is designed so that your data never leaves your device.

## What the app uses

- **Motion sensors.** While a session is running, Posture Help reads your iPhone's orientation (CoreMotion) to estimate how far you are leaning forward, and whether the phone is in your hand, locked, in a pocket, or resting on a surface. These readings are processed in real time on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device. Reminder text may be generated by Apple's on-device language model. Nothing is sent to a server.
- **On-device storage.** Your settings, calibration and daily posture totals (minutes tracked, upright score and number of reminders) are saved on your device so you can see your history and widgets can display your status.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Posture Help has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data.

## Your control

- You can turn notifications or motion access off at any time in the iOS Settings app.
- **Settings → Reset History** in the app deletes your stored posture history.
- Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

Posture Help does not collect data from anyone, including children.
